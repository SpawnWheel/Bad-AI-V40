### Previous Race Summary: Donington Park (Super Touring Week 1)
In the season opener at Donington Park, **Daniel Born** drove his rear-wheel-drive **BMW** through a chaotic 26-car field to finish **13th**. His race was defined by fiery midfield combat, contrasting drivetrain battles, and tactical tyre management.

---

### Key Driver Rivalries to Highlight

#### 1. Dominikos Moustakas (Esso Peugeot) — *The "Drift Taxi" Coffee Bet*
* **The Backstory:** In pre-season testing, Moustakas mocked Born's rear-wheel-drive BMW as a "drift taxi". The two placed a wager on this race with Born's monthly **coffee ration** on the line.
* **On-Track Clashes:** After serving an early cut-track penalty, Moustakas charged back into Born's mirrors, sparking a ten-minute dogfight over 14th and 15th position. 
* **Radio Banter:** When Moustakas passed Born into Redgate, he taunted on team radio: *"Enjoy P15, drift taxi!"*, to which Born immediately retorted: *"Enjoy your front tyres!"*
* **The Climax:** Born's prediction came true. Moustakas severely overheated his front-left tyre under braking, locked up into the Redgate gravel trap, and gifted the spot back. Born crossed the line in 13th while Moustakas finished 15th, meaning Moustakas owes Born a month's supply of espresso.
* **Upcoming Race Angle:** If Moustakas and Born share tarmac again, commentators can lean heavily on the unpaid coffee debt, bruised pride, and whether Moustakas seeks payback.

#### 2. Jona Mingo (Ford Mondeo) — *"Find Your Own Tarmac"*
* **The Conflict:** Chasing 13th place, Born closed down Mingo's front-wheel-drive Ford Mondeo, using superior rear-wheel traction off the apex of the Old Hairpin. Under braking and side-by-side through the chicane, Born pleaded over the radio: *"Move the Mondeo, mate!"*
* **Mingo's Slammed Door:** Mingo fired back over the radio: *"Find your own tarmac!"*, refusing to leave an inch on exit and forcing Born wide.
* **The Fallout:** Losing momentum in the chicane scrap left Born vulnerable down the straight, allowing **Pepe Rodriguez's Volvo** to sweep past and drop Born to 15th. Mingo pulled clear to take 10th.
* **Upcoming Race Angle:** Born will feel aggrieved by Mingo's aggressive door-slamming and the lost positions it caused. Expect fireworks if Born finds himself tucked behind the Mondeo again.

#### 3. Secondary Midfield Skirmishes
* **Pepe Rodriguez (Volvo):** Capitalized instantly on Born's stalled momentum behind Mingo, mugging Born down the straight for position.
* **Ostenstad:** On lap one, Ostenstad briefly passed Born for 15th, but Born immediately threw his elbows out and snatched the position right back.

---

### Core Commentator Talking Points for Next Race

1. **Rear-Wheel Drive vs. Front-Wheel Drive Dynamics:** 
   Born's BMW has noticeably superior launch and traction off slow corner apexes compared to heavy-nosed front-drive cars like Mingo's Mondeo and Moustakas's Peugeot. However, over-sliding in heavy traffic overheats Born's rear tyres and causes him to lose drive onto long straights.
2. **Patience Under Pressure:** 
   While Born drives aggressively in wheel-to-wheel combat, Donington proved he can also play the long game—waiting out Moustakas until the Peugeot's tyres disintegrated rather than taking desperate lunges.
3. **Midfield Recovery & Pace:** 
   Born climbed from the mid-pack up to 13th, surviving wheel-to-wheel scraps without picking up penalties or terminal damage. His target for the next round will be breaking into the top 10.
